A clear process

How shipment coordination works

From arranging a collection to completing a delivery, we aim to make every stage straightforward. Our team provides shipment information and updates to help customers follow their deliveries.

STEP 01

Share shipment details

Provide the collection point, destination, package information and contact details.

STEP 02

Confirm arrangements

Our team reviews the request and confirms the available service, schedule and applicable charges.

STEP 03

Receive your reference

Once a shipment is registered, use the assigned tracking reference to check its recorded progress.

STEP 04

Follow delivery updates

Check status updates and delivery information as they are entered by the logistics team.
